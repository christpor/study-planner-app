## What is Study Planner?

Study Planner is a simple and beautiful app that helps you organize your learning. Whether you want to learn a new skill, prepare for an exam, or master a subject, this app makes it easy to break down big goals into small tasks and watch your progress grow.

### Key Features

- **Set Learning Goals** — Create goals like "Learn React in 30 days" or "Master Public Speaking"
- **Break into Tasks** — Split each goal into smaller, manageable tasks
- **Track Progress** — See how many tasks you have completed and celebrate your wins
- **Offline Support** — Works even without internet connection
- **Install as App** — Use it on your phone or desktop like a native app
- **Modern Design** — Beautiful, easy-to-use interface built for 2026

## Getting Started

### Visit the App

Open [Study Planner](https://studyplan-52f69726.netlify.app) in your web browser. No installation needed!

### Create Your First Goal

1. Click the **"+"** button at the bottom right
2. Enter your learning goal (e.g., "Learn Python Basics")
3. Set a target date
4. Click **"Create Goal"**

### Add Tasks to Your Goal

1. Open your goal
2. Click **"Add Task"**
3. Enter task details (e.g., "Complete Chapter 1")
4. Set difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard)
5. Click **"Create Task"**

### Track Your Progress

1. Check off tasks as you complete them
2. Watch your progress bar grow
3. View your streak on the **Progress** tab
4. Celebrate when you complete a goal!

## Features Explained

### Goals

A goal is your big learning target. Examples:
- Learn a new programming language
- Prepare for a certification exam
- Master a musical instrument
- Improve a skill

Each goal shows:
- How many tasks are completed
- Your progress percentage
- Days remaining until your target date

### Tasks

Tasks are the small steps you take to reach your goal. Each task has:
- A title and description
- Difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard)
- Priority level (Low, Medium, High)
- Due date
- Completion status

### Progress Dashboard

The Progress tab shows:
- Total goals created
- Tasks completed today
- Your current streak (days in a row with completed tasks)
- Overall completion rate

## How to Use Offline

Study Planner works offline! Here's how:

1. **First Visit** — Open the app and create some goals and tasks
2. **Go Offline** — Turn off your internet or use airplane mode
3. **Keep Using** — All your data is saved locally on your device
4. **Sync When Online** — When you connect to internet again, your data is safe

### Test Offline Mode

1. Open the app
2. Open DevTools (Press F12)
3. Go to **Application** tab
4. Check **Offline** box
5. Refresh the page
6. The app still works!

## Troubleshooting

### The app won't load

- Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
- Try a different browser
- Check your internet connection

### My data disappeared

- Check if you're using a private/incognito window (data won't save there)
- Try opening the app in a normal window
- Your data is saved locally, so it should still be there

### Offline mode not working

- Make sure the app loaded at least once while online
- Check if service workers are enabled in your browser
- Try refreshing the page while offline
